## Aurora MarvinS

Aurora MarvinS is an unofficial continuation/modification maintained by **Sanek** for Aurora 4X C# 2.7.1.

The original author gave permission for the project to be continued and published as free and open source and agreed to the plan to publish Aurora MarvinS under the GNU General Public License v3.0.

Aurora MarvinS is not the official Aurora 4X application.
